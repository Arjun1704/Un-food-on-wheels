Our Story

Built on a hunch, cooked with heart.

The name is a joke and a promise. "Unपढ़" — unlettered — because this cart never went to culinary school and never plans to. What it does have is a recipe book written in family kitchens, a tawa that's always hot, and two guys who'd rather feed a street full of people than fill out a menu with fancy French words.

Founders Mayank Choudhary and Arjun Choudhary started with one simple idea: park an e-rickshaw at the busiest corner in town, load it with the food everyone actually craves, and never make anyone wait more than a few minutes for it. No AC dining room, no service charge, no attitude — just honest, loaded, street-style food that shows up wherever the wheels take it.

On The Cart Today

Two dishes. Zero compromise.

We're launching small and loud — two dishes we've perfected over countless late-night test batches. Everything else on this page is what's coming next.

Aloo Tikki Burger

₹30

A crisp, hand-pressed potato-and-spice tikki, double-fried till the crust cracks, stacked with tangy green chutney, sweet-and-sour imli, a slap of masala mayo, fresh onions and a slice of melting cheese — all crammed into a soft toasted bun. It's the burger your local dhaba would invent if it ever went to America.

Veg Chowmin

₹30

Wok-tossed noodles on a screaming-hot tawa, loaded with shredded cabbage, carrot, capsicum and spring onion, finished with a smoky desi masala that only comes from cooking on an open flame at the side of the road. Fast, fiery, and made to order.
